Nomadic Culture of Mongolia

Mongolia is famous for its nomadic lifestyle that has been preserved for centuries. Families move across the steppe following seasonal changes.

Traditional homes called gers are portable and designed for extreme climates. Hospitality is very important in Mongolian culture.

The Naadam Festival is the biggest cultural event featuring wrestling, horse racing, and archery.
