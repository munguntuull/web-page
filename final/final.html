Welcome to Mongolia

Mongolia is a country of endless blue skies, vast steppes, deserts, and mountains. It is known for its unique nomadic heritage and rich cultural traditions that continue to this day.

The country offers a rare opportunity to experience untouched nature and traditional lifestyles that have existed for centuries.
