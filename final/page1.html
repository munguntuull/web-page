About Mongolia

Mongolia is a landlocked country located between Russia and China in Central Asia. It is one of the least densely populated countries in the world.

Its history is deeply connected to the Mongol Empire, founded by Genghis Khan in the 13th century, which became one of the largest empires in history.

Today, Mongolia combines modern urban life in Ulaanbaatar with traditional nomadic culture across rural regions.

Source: Britannica Mongolia
